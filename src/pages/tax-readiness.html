What students will learn

Five modules. One clear outcome.

  • CTC vs take-home
  • Salary components: basic, HRA, allowances
  • Deductions: PF, professional tax, TDS
  • Reading your payslip line by line
  • How income tax works in India
  • Taxable income vs total income
  • What PAN is and why it matters
  • Where to check current rules each year
  • Choosing a tax regime with your employer
  • Declaring investments and rent
  • Keeping the right proofs
  • TDS (tax taken before you're paid) and Form 16
  • Form 26AS and AIS
  • Filing your ITR step by step
  • Refunds and what to do if something's wrong
  • Common tax scams
  • Fake refund messages
  • Using the official portal safely
  • Protecting your PAN
