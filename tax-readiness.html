Your First Salary. Your First Tax. Know What To Do.

4 HoursFor College StudentsPlacement Cell Program

Your offer letter says one number. Your bank account says another. In one interactive workshop, final-year students learn how salary, tax, TDS and ITR actually work, before their first payslip arrives.

No tax jargon. No long lectures.

What students will learn

Five modules. One clear outcome.

  • CTC vs take-home
  • Salary components: basic, HRA, allowances
  • Deductions: PF, professional tax, TDS
  • Reading your payslip line by line
  • How income tax works in India
  • Taxable income vs total income
  • What PAN is and why it matters
  • Where to check current rules each year
  • Choosing a tax regime with your employer
  • Declaring investments and rent
  • Keeping the right proofs
  • TDS (tax taken before you're paid) and Form 16
  • Form 26AS and AIS
  • Filing your ITR step by step
  • Refunds and what to do if something's wrong
  • Common tax scams
  • Fake refund messages
  • Using the official portal safely
  • Protecting your PAN

Students will be able to…

  • Explain the difference between CTC and take-home pay
  • Read every line of a payslip
  • Understand how income tax is calculated
  • Choose a tax regime with a clear reason

  • Spot common tax scams and fake refund messages
  • Keep their PAN and tax records safe
