The 5-stage journey

Earn a stamp at every stage.

Each stage builds on the last. By the end, learners have a full passport and a plan they can act on.

Stage 1 of 5
Stage 01 · Awareness

Money Matters

Why money decisions matter now, not someday, and how they shape the life you want.

What you'll do: map where your money comes from and where it goes in a normal month.

Stage 02 · Understanding

Personal Finance Essentials

Budgets, banking, credit, saving and protection, explained in plain words.

What you'll do: build your first budget and read a real bank statement.

Stage 03 · Experience

Applied Finance for Work & Life

Simulations of the moments that matter: a first salary, a loan offer, a big purchase.

What you'll do: run a month of adult life in a money simulation.

Stage 04 · Application

Solve Real-World Finance Problems

Case studies and team challenges based on real situations young people face.

What you'll do: crack a case, from a scam message to a BNPL trap.

Stage 05 · Action

Finance in Action

Learners take what they know into a project for their family, campus or community.

What you'll do: plan and present a money project that helps real people.

Curriculum

What will a learner learn?

Seven themes cover the money decisions of the next ten years. Tap a theme to see its topics.

  • Needs vs wants and building a budget
  • UPI and digital payments, safely
  • Tracking spending and saving goals
  • Pocket money, stipends and PG rent
  • Bank accounts, debit cards and statements
  • Credit cards, credit scores and how they are built
  • Loans, EMI and interest
  • BNPL offers: what to check first
  • Emergency funds
  • Insurance basics: health, life and more
  • Online scams, fake links and OTP fraud
  • Protecting your personal and financial data
  • Risk and return
  • Compounding and time
  • SIPs, mutual funds and how they work
  • Spotting “get rich quick” schemes
  • Why we pay tax and where it goes
  • PAN and your tax identity
  • TDS (tax taken before you're paid)
  • Filing an ITR, step by step
  • Setting short- and long-term goals
  • Paying for education
  • Your first salary and first big purchases
  • Planning for the future, early
  • Pricing, costs and profit
  • Cash flow for small businesses
  • Funding basics
  • Digital tools for business
